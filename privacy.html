At a glance

Caldrixa processes the ABI and calldata you provide on your device. The app does not require an account, connect to a wallet, fetch blockchain data, or transmit your inputs to a Caldrixa server. You decide when to copy, save, or share a report.

Overview

This policy describes the Caldrixa iOS app and the Caldrixa support website. It is effective November 13, 2026. If the app or website changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated.

Data in the iOS app

You may provide ABI JSON, names for saved ABIs, and calldata. The app uses this information to match function selectors, decode supported arguments, compare calls, and prepare reports.

  • Saved ABIs: ABI names and JSON you choose to save are stored locally on your device.
  • Calldata: Inputs are processed on your device. They are included in a report only when you choose to create, copy, save, or share one.
  • Built-in example: The example ABI and calldata are packaged with the app and do not require a network request.

The app does not include a Caldrixa account, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or a service that receives your ABI or calldata. It does not request access to a wallet or submit transactions.

Files, clipboard, and sharing

When you import a file, the app reads the file you select through the iOS file picker. When you copy text, it is placed on the device clipboard. When you save or share a report, iOS passes the selected content to the destination you choose. Those destinations, including file providers and sharing apps, may have their own privacy practices.

Reports contain the supplied calldata and available decoded values. The complete ABI is not included by default. Review a report before sharing it, especially if its input contains information you do not want to disclose.

This website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics or advertising scripts to these pages. GitHub may process website access information, including IP addresses, as the hosting provider. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

If you email support, your email address and message are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending sensitive ABI or calldata unless needed to explain the issue. Email delivery and storage are also subject to your email provider’s practices.

Contact and questions

For privacy questions or requests concerning a support message you sent, contact caldrixa.support@gmail.com. For product help, visit the support page.
